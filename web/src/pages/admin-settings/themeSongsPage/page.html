Theme songs

Themes for TV shows and films from Plex's public TV theme collection, then ThemerrDB's community YouTube links, then (if you switch it on) a YouTube search you approve. Kept in Silo's own storage; your media folders are never written to.

"To review" holds YouTube search results. Listen first, then keep the theme or reject it; rejecting searches again for the next result.
